    *   Understand how software (Dorico/Sibelius) reads MusicXML for *playback*. How to write *expression maps*, *key switches*, and *CC11/CC1 automation* inside the score so that the orchestra *mockup* sounds realistic.


### 2. Specific Engraving Rules
> *"According to Elaine Gould (Behind Bars, p. 387), harmonic symbols must be written with a diamond-shaped (◇) notehead, and if there are two pitches (base pitch and sounding pitch), the base pitch is written with a normal notehead below and the sounding pitch with a diamond above."*

### 3. Edge Cases and Troubleshooting
> *"If the notation software (Dorico/Sibelius) does not render the diamond notehead correctly when importing MusicXML, add the `smufl="noteheadDiamond"` attribute to the `<notehead>` tag to force the correct glyph."*
